We've been talking a lot about AI, AI, and listening.

But not for the sake of technology.

We use AI to listen so we can create **better conversations internally**. Because at the end of the day, it's not about the data — it's about what happens between people because of it. Stronger teams. Better alignment. Real connection.

We're often asked by leaders: _"What are some of your favorite short team huddle ice breakers?"_

Here you go!

1. **What's one word to describe how you're showing up today?** Fast. Simple. Tells you more than a status update ever will.
2. **What's one win — big or small — from this week?** Momentum matters. This shifts the room instantly.
3. **What's taking more energy than it should?** A quiet way to surface friction before it becomes a problem.
4. **What's one thing you need right now?** Clear. Actionable. Often overlooked.
5. **If we only solved one thing today, what should it be?** Cuts through noise and aligns everyone quickly.

### Why This Matters

Ice breakers aren't about being clever. They're about **creating space for people to show up as humans before they show up as roles**.

And when that happens, even for a few minutes, everything changes:

- Conversations get more honest
- Problems surface faster
- Teams move with more clarity

That's not a "soft" outcome. That's how better organizations operate.

### Final Thought

The best teams don't need longer meetings. They need better starts.

Try one of these in your next huddle. Two minutes. Real impact.
